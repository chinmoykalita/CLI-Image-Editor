What the execution gap costs the payer

$300B

is the upper-bound estimate of what US healthcare spends every year because prescribed plans are not executed at home.

Published range: $100B–$300B annually. Hospitals, insurers and risk-bearing groups absorb it as avoidable admissions, readmission penalties and repeat utilisation.

~1 in 10
US hospitalisations are linked to medication non-adherence.
~125,000
deaths a year are associated with non-adherence.
~2,545
hospitals carry a Medicare readmission penalty in FY2026.

Sources: Viswanathan et al., Annals of Internal Medicine (2012); CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program, FY2026. Estimates are contested and directional — kuvos does not claim to recover this figure.
